<Summary>
- No provider wins on speed, price and reach at once. **QuickNode is the fastest and most consistent private RPC we measure, BlockMachine the cheapest and the slowest, and Chainstack the best balance of the two.**
- QuickNode has the fastest HTTP median in 18 of 27 chain and region cells and the best p99 in 21 of 27, with a median p99 of 9.2 ms. It pushes new blocks first over WebSocket in 13 of 18 cells.
- <Mark>Region matters more than provider.</Mark> Chainstack is 4x slower from Singapore than from Virginia, and Dwellir answers in about 75 ms from Virginia on six of its eight chains.
- Price spreads are wider than speed spreads: the same 100M dapp requests cost $125 to $1,255 a month across our six providers.
- BlockMachine is cheapest on 4 of 5 Ethereum workloads at 100M and 1B requests ($125, $999). Dwellir is cheapest for trace and debug ($299 at 100M).
- A free tier is a trial: the Alchemy Free key we probe with ran out of its 30M CU on 24 October, and every call on seven chains has failed since.
</Summary>

## Scope and method

The report combines four OpenChainBench measurements of six private (API-key) providers. Speed figures cover the 7 days to 28 October 2026, 13:00 UTC. Prices are list prices from the pricing catalogue, last verified 22 October.

| Bench | What it measures |
|---|---|
| [Chain RPC pages, private cohort](/benchmarks/ethereum-rpc?tier=keyed) | HTTP round trip for `eth_getBlockByNumber` (`getSlot` on Solana) every 120 s per chain and region. Two columns: warm (pooled connection) and cold (new DNS, TCP and TLS every call). Every answer is classed ok, http_err, jsonrpc_err, stale or timeout. |
| [WebSocket push race](/benchmarks/base-rpc-ws) | One `eth_subscribe(newHeads)` socket per provider and region, 10 minutes in every hour. Each block is scored against the first provider to push it in that region. |
| [rpc-cost, bench 282](/benchmarks/rpc-cost) | Monthly bill of five fixed method mixes at any volume, from each provider's published unit weights. |
| [rpc-free-tier, bench 283](/benchmarks/rpc-free-tier) | Requests a renewing free plan buys, per workload. |

**Providers and plans.** Plans are disclosed on every bench page; QuickNode, Chainstack and GetBlock supplied their endpoint.

| Provider | Plan probed | Chains served of our 9 | Region handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| QuickNode | Build ($49), endpoint supplied | 9 | One routed URL per chain |
| Chainstack | Growth Global Node, endpoint supplied | 9 | One routed URL per chain |
| GetBlock | Enterprise, endpoint supplied | 9 (no Singapore node for Arc and Robinhood) | One endpoint per region: New York, Frankfurt, Singapore |
| Alchemy | Free key (30M CU a month) on 7 chains, enterprise key on HyperEVM and Robinhood | 9 | One routed URL per chain |
| Dwellir | Developer ($49, 25M responses) | 8 (no Solana) | One routed URL per chain |
| BlockMachine | Standard (5M Request Units) | 7 (no Solana, no HyperEVM) | One routed URL per chain |

**Chains:** Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Solana, HyperEVM, Robinhood Chain, Arc. **Regions:** US East (Virginia), EU West (Amsterdam), Singapore.

**How to read the numbers.** Latency here is the 7-day median and 99th percentile of raw probe samples, steadier than the 24-hour figure the bench pages headline. Warm is what a long-running server sees; cold is what a serverless function or a fresh script sees. Validation Cloud left the cohort on 18 October: its key returned 401 on every call.

## HTTP latency and reliability

QuickNode is the only provider that stays under 10 ms at p50 and about 10 ms at p99 in all three regions. Every other provider has at least one region where it falls behind. The table gives the median across the chains each provider serves.

- **QuickNode.** Its tail is the tightest: p99 is within 3 ms of p50 almost everywhere. Two exceptions: Arc and Robinhood from Singapore (163 ms and 66 ms), which QuickNode serves from outside Asia, and Ethereum from Singapore at p99 (176 ms).
- **Chainstack.** The fastest provider from US East, at 2.4 to 3.1 ms on six chains. From Singapore it degrades on Ethereum (39 ms), Polygon (83 ms), HyperEVM (70 ms) and Robinhood (39 ms). Its HyperEVM node also answers in 63 ms from US East.
- **GetBlock.** It never posts the fastest median, but it is close everywhere and has the most even cold start, because it gives each region its own endpoint. Two outliers: HyperEVM from US East (235 ms) and Polygon p99 from US East (1.3 s). It had 88 stale answers in 7 days, the most in the cohort.
- **Alchemy.** Its 55% success rate reflects our plan, not its network: the Free key hit its monthly cap around 24 October and has returned HTTP errors on 7 chains since. On HyperEVM and Robinhood, where we hold an enterprise key, it scored 100% and was the fastest from Singapore on Robinhood. Its latency columns cover only the days the key worked.
- **Dwellir.** It is fast from Singapore on five chains (3.2 to 3.9 ms) but takes about 177 ms there on Arbitrum, Polygon and Robinhood. From US East it answers in about 75 ms on six of its eight chains; Base and Robinhood are the exceptions (2.4 to 2.7 ms). From EU West its p90 jumps to 150 ms. Cold start costs 113 to 330 ms.
- **BlockMachine.** It is the slowest in every region. Its best region is EU West (42 ms), which suggests a single European location. It had the most timeouts (153 in 7 days); its p99 reaches 2.4 s on BNB from US East.

## Cost at each budget

At every volume, the gap between the cheapest and the dearest of our six providers is 5x to 13x. Speed does not track price: the fastest provider, QuickNode, is the second dearest from 100M requests up.

**Monthly bill for the dapp mix on Ethereum** (40% `eth_call`; 15% each balances, block number and receipts; 5% each gas estimates, sends and logs), cheapest paid plan that can serve the volume, list prices:

| Dwellir | $49 Developer | $49 Developer | $174 Developer | $299 Growth | $1,999 Scale |

|---|---|---|---|

| Solana trading bot | Chainstack, dRPC, Helius $49 | Syndica $199 | Syndica $1,362 |

What moves these bills:

- **Unit weights decide the ranking, not headline prices.** Dwellir and BlockMachine bill one unit per response, archive included. Alchemy charges 26 CU for one `eth_call`, so the dapp mix costs 23.9 CU a request. QuickNode charges 20 credits per Ethereum call and 30 on Solana.
- **Trace breaks BlockMachine's lead.** It meters a trace call at 25 Request Units, so trace at 10M costs $429 there against $49 at Dwellir or Chainstack. GetBlock ($515) and QuickNode ($223) are also expensive for trace.
- **Chainstack is a step function.** $49 covers 20M Request Units, which is exactly 10M archive requests at 2 RU each. At 10.1M it is $52, at 11M $79, at 15M $199. Budget with headroom.
- **Throughput caps bite before the bill does.** Alchemy pay-as-you-go stops at 30,000 CU a second, so it cannot serve 500M indexer or trace requests a month at any price. BlockMachine's 4,167 RU a second cap stops trace at the same volume.
- **Solana narrows the field.** Dwellir and BlockMachine do not price Solana. Among our six, Chainstack is cheapest from 10M up ($49, $449, $3,990); QuickNode costs 3.3x to 3.6x more from 100M up.

All figures are published list prices, not invoices. Alchemy and QuickNode expose no usage header, so their billed units cannot be checked against our traffic. dRPC prices are an upper bound, because it routes to third-party operators who may charge less. The calculator on [rpc-cost](/benchmarks/rpc-cost) prices any volume from 100,000 to 5B requests.

- **Allowance is not throughput.** GetBlock's 50,000 CU a day lasts 125 seconds at its own advertised 20 requests a second. Infura's daily credits last 1.7 hours at full rate, OnFinality's 2.8 hours.

- **Three allowances are unverified.** dRPC publishes its 210M CU but not what happens when it runs out. Blockdaemon's own pages give 1M or 3M CU. BlockMachine's pages disagree on its rate limit and on whether archive is free.

| Team based in Asia | QuickNode | GetBlock | Both under 4 ms p50 from Singapore on most chains. Chainstack and Dwellir are uneven there. |
| Prototype, no budget | Chainstack free, dRPC free | Alchemy free | 3M and 10.5M dapp requests a month. Expect to outgrow any of them within weeks of real traffic. |

**Caveats.** Our probes run in three cloud regions and send one cheap call; a heavy method or another city can change the order. Warm latency below about 10 ms mostly reflects distance to the nearest node. QuickNode, Chainstack and GetBlock supplied their endpoints; Alchemy, Dwellir and BlockMachine run on keys we hold, at the plans disclosed above. Prices are list prices and do not include negotiated discounts.
